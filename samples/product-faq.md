# StarSync Product FAQ

Sample data. StarSync is Sao Bien Company's (fictional) file synchronization product.

## What is StarSync?

StarSync is a desktop and mobile application that keeps folders synchronized across devices and with the company cloud. It supports Windows 10+, macOS 13+, Ubuntu 22.04+, Android 12+ and iOS 16+.

## Pricing plans

- **Free**: 5 GB storage, up to 2 devices.
- **Pro**: 1 TB storage, unlimited devices, 30-day file version history, priced at 99,000 VND per month.
- **Business**: 5 TB pooled storage per team, 180-day version history, admin console and SSO, priced at 199,000 VND per user per month (minimum 5 users).

Annual billing gives a 20% discount on Pro and Business plans.

## How do I restore a deleted file?

Open the StarSync web console, go to **Trash**, select the file and click **Restore**. Deleted files are kept for 30 days on Pro and 180 days on Business. Free plan files are kept in trash for 7 days.

## Is my data encrypted?

Yes. Files are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Business customers can enable end-to-end encryption, in which case the company cannot recover files if the encryption key is lost.

## How do I get support?

Free users can use the community forum. Pro users get email support with a 48-hour response time. Business customers get 24/7 support with a 4-hour response time for critical incidents via support@starsync.example.
