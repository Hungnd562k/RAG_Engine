# Remote Work Policy

Sample data for testing the RAG engine. Applies to all full-time employees of Sao Bien Company.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period are eligible to work remotely. Remote work is not available for roles that require on-site presence, such as reception, facilities and hardware lab staff.

## Hybrid schedule

The default arrangement is hybrid: employees work from the office at least 2 days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday as team anchor days. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department director and are reviewed every 6 months.

## Equipment and allowance

Remote employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 3,000,000 VND to purchase a chair, desk or monitor, plus a monthly internet allowance of 200,000 VND. Company laptops must not be shared with family members.

## Working abroad

Working from another country for up to 30 calendar days per year is allowed with manager approval, provided the employee stays in a time zone within 3 hours of Vietnam (UTC+7). Longer stays require approval from HR and Legal due to tax and labor law implications.

## Communication expectations

Remote employees must be reachable on the company chat during core hours (10:00–16:00, UTC+7), keep their calendar up to date, and turn on their camera during team meetings unless bandwidth does not allow it.
